About Me

I'm Shuo Liu, an undergraduate at Henan University of Economics and Law, where I work with Ming Zhang. I'm also a visiting student at Zhengzhou University, advised by Prof. Qidong Liu, and I study with Prof. Changcheng Zhao with the aim of applying deep learning to problems in biology.

My main interest lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and operations research, particularly AI for Combinatorial Optimization (AI4CO). Outside research, I enjoy engineering topics such as AI infrastructure, AI application development, compilers, and operating systems.
